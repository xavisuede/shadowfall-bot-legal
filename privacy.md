# SHADOWFALL BOT — PRIVACY POLICY

**Effective Date:** October 31, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 31, 2026

## 1. Overview

Shadowfall Bot is a Discord application developed to support the Shadowfall Dune: Awakening gaming guild. It provides server organization, guild ranks, merit tracking, bounty contracts, event coordination, and member management.

This policy explains how information is handled when members use Shadowfall Bot.

## 2. Information Collected

Depending on which features are enabled, the bot may process:

- Discord user IDs and server IDs.
- Discord usernames and assigned server roles.
- Guild rank, Merit Points, and promotion history.
- Bounty participation, submissions, verification status, and rewards.
- Guild operation announcements and related participation records.
- Information members voluntarily submit through bot commands.

The bot does not require passwords, payment card details, or real-world identity documents.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate guild features, administer promotions, track earned rewards, prevent misuse, maintain guild records, and respond to member requests.

Information is not sold to advertisers.

## 4. Storage and Security

Bot records may be stored in a database controlled by the bot operator. Access is restricted to authorized administrators and technical personnel.

Reasonable security measures will be used to protect records. No storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

## 5. Third-Party Services

The bot operates through Discord and is subject to Discord's own policies. Other bots separately installed in the Shadowfall server may have independent privacy practices.

## 6. Data Retention

Records are retained while needed for guild administration, operational continuity, and dispute resolution. Data that is no longer necessary will be deleted or anonymized when reasonably practicable.

## 7. Access and Deletion Requests

Members may request information about their stored bot records or request deletion by contacting Shadowfall server administration through an administrative support ticket.

Some records may be retained temporarily where necessary to resolve an outstanding dispute or satisfy legal obligations.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Shadowfall Bot is intended for users who meet Discord's minimum age requirements and applicable laws. The bot is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children who are not eligible to use Discord.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to bot functionality or data practices. Material changes will be communicated through the Shadowfall Discord server.

## 10. Contact

**Operator:** Shadowfall Guild Administration  
**Contact Method:** Administrative support ticket in the official Shadowfall Discord server.
